About Zuri Beauty Academy

Established in December 2014 under the stewardship of Morph Academy, Beauty Academy brings with it a legacy of three decades of experience in vocational education. As a private training organization, our commitment lies in providing Nationally Recognized Training in a range of certificate, diploma, and degree courses.
